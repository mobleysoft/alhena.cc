# Cove Impact Feedback: 2026-09-24

## Module And Integration

`public/island/splash.js` contains `createSpray`, a bounded ballistic particle
simulation, and `createSplashes`, its Three.js renderer. `main.js` connects
casts, strikes, fish jumps and catch-release events to this module. There is
no new network service, asset dependency, or background process.

- A maximum of 160 droplets is allocated once and rendered with one instanced
  mesh. Six reusable splash crowns follow the current water surface.
- Droplets use gravity and horizontal drag, collide with the same ocean
  height used for buoyancy, and return small bounded impulses to the existing
  ripple field. Contacts in one simulation step are aggregated. Secondary
  impulses never create more particles, preventing recursive amplification.
- Dry-land and nonfinite impacts are rejected. Lifetime limits bound spray
  even if the sampled water surface moves out of reach.
- Crown curvature, droplets and sunlit highlights remain in the existing
  miniature art direction. Color follows the selected environment.
- Reduced-motion mode disables airborne spray, while retaining the primary
  water impulse. Audio remains opt-in; impacts layer a filtered noise decay
  over a descending low-frequency oscillator. Completed audio nodes disconnect.

## Explicit Limits

Spray trajectories run on the CPU; the receiving ripple field uses WebGPU
where available and the tested CPU fallback otherwise. This is not a full
3D fluid simulation, volumetric refraction, or a WebGPU rendering migration.
The crowns are time-shaped impact meshes, not a solved fluid sheet. Actual
phone motion, haptics, thermals and frame rate still require hardware testing.
